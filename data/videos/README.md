# Sample Videos Directory

## Overview
This directory contains sample video files for testing the people counting demo.

## Required Videos

You need some videos of people walking. Good sources:

### Option 1: Free Stock Video Sites
- **Pexels Videos**: https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/people%20walking/
- **Pixabay**: https://pixabay.com/videos/search/people%20walking/
- **Videvo**: https://www.videvo.net/free-stock-video-footage/people-walking/

### Option 2: Record Your Own
- Use your phone to record people walking
- Ensure good lighting and stable camera position

### Option 3: Public Datasets
- **MOT Challenge**: https://motchallenge.net/ (pedestrian tracking datasets)
- **PETS Dataset**: http://www.cvg.reading.ac.uk/PETS2009/

## Recommended Specifications

- **Format**: MP4, AVI, or MOV
- **Resolution**: 720p or 1080p
- **Frame Rate**: 15-30 FPS
- **Duration**: 30 seconds to 2 minutes
- **Content**: Clear view of people walking, preferably from a fixed camera angle
